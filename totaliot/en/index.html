Sectors

Which challenges we solve.

Sectors where IoT adds direct value by making comfort, efficiency, insight or sustainability measurable.

Smart Building

Comfort, air quality, occupancy and energy use in offices, schools and healthcare facilities. Made visible and controllable.

Smart Energy

Monitoring and control of energy flows, kWh meters, battery systems and charging infrastructure. Insight into where the tipping point lies.

Smart Water

Level, flow and quality measurements for water authorities, operators and municipalities. From a single sensor to a dashboard with alerting.

Smart Industry

Modbus, OPC-UA and industrial sensors in production, utilities and facility management. Retrofitting of existing installations.

Smart Asset Tracking

Location and condition monitoring of mobile assets: vehicles, containers, tools. Cellular or LoRa, depending on what fits.

Building blocks

What a solution is built from.

Every IoT project combines the same fundamentals in a different mix. These are the blocks I work with.

Data integration

API integrations or retrofitting of existing systems. Connecting sensors, machines or legacy installations to a modern data stream.

Sensors

Selection, placement and configuration of LoRa and cellular sensors, from temperature and CO₂ to energy, water level and occupancy.

LoRaWAN

Site surveys, gateway deployment and payload decoding. Your own network or via TTN, KPN Things or a comparable platform.

Cellular (4G/5G/NB-IoT)

Industrial routers, VPN tunnels, remote management. For situations where LoRa does not fit because of bandwidth, location or robustness.

Data visualisation

Custom dashboards: not yet another Grafana screenshot, but an interface that fits how your team makes decisions.

Automation

Triggers, alerts and business rules, so data does not sit idle in a dashboard but leads to action, notification or control.

About Total IoT

Small, but fully end-to-end.

Total IoT is an independent IoT practice based in the Netherlands. Not a company that only sells hardware or only builds dashboards, but a single point of contact for the entire project: from the first sensor to the end result in a report or dashboard.

Independent and therefore agile: short lines, no overhead, and the freedom to choose the hardware and platform that truly fit best. Larger projects are delivered together with trusted partners.

Clients range from water authorities to property managers to industrial installers. What they have in common: they want to measure or control something that is not yet visible.
